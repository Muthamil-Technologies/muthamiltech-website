JapTalk

A speaking-first Japanese learning app for adults with a real reason to speak Japanese.

Most language apps front-load memorization — vocabulary decks, grammar drills — before ever asking the learner to produce a sentence. JapTalk inverts that: every lesson opens directly with a speaking challenge, typed or spoken, and grammar/vocabulary reference is available on demand but never gates the practice.

Content is organized into five goal-based Learning Paths instead of abstract JLPT levels:

  • Beginner Foundations — the essentials, from scratch
  • Everyday Japan — shopping, cafés, transportation, daily errands
  • Everyday Conversation — making plans, sharing opinions, handling problems
  • Business Japanese — meetings, workplace requests, customer conversations
  • Interview Japanese — self-introductions and a full mock interview

Runs fully offline, no account required, no ads.

JapTest

Focused JLPT preparation for learners who need to pass a specific level.

Where JapTalk is about real-world speaking, JapTest is unapologetically about the exam: the JLPT's actual format, question types, and time pressure. Study time goes toward what the test asks for, not generic vocabulary review disconnected from how the JLPT actually questions you.

Planned to share JapTalk's underlying philosophy — no bloat, no forced detours — applied to a very different goal: a passing score on a specific date.

JapJob

Clear, practical information about working in Japan.

Visa categories, what Japanese hiring actually looks for, and how to find roles that sponsor and hire from abroad — the practical layer that sits after someone can already speak Japanese and is ready to work there. Built for the moment JapTalk's "why" (a real-world goal) turns into an actual job search.

JapTour

Trip planning guidance for travel in Japan.

Starts with the practical core: building an itinerary that actually fits how you want to travel — pace, region, budget, and interests — rather than a generic top-10 list. Guided tours are a natural next step, but that requires proper licensing to operate, so JapTour begins with planning guidance only.
